Acme Cloud Services - Company Handbook (Sample)

1. About the Company
Acme Cloud Services was founded in 2015 in Austin, Texas by Maria Lopez and David Chen. The company provides cloud hosting, managed databases, and DevOps consulting to mid-sized businesses. As of 2025, Acme has 240 employees across offices in Austin, London, and Singapore.

2. Products
- AcmeHost: virtual machine hosting with 99.95% uptime SLA. Plans start at $20 per month.
- AcmeDB: managed PostgreSQL and MySQL databases with automatic daily backups retained for 14 days.
- AcmeDeploy: a CI/CD platform that integrates with GitHub and GitLab.

3. Support Policy
Support is available 24/7 via chat and email. Priority customers receive a phone line with a guaranteed 15-minute response time. Standard tickets are answered within 4 business hours. Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for annual plans.

4. Leave and Benefits
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year and 10 days of sick leave. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid. Employees get a $1,000 annual learning budget and may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

5. Security Practices
All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Acme is SOC 2 Type II certified and completes a third-party penetration test every year. Security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within 1 hour of discovery.

6. Roadmap for 2026
- Q1: Launch AcmeDB support for MongoDB.
- Q2: Open a new data center in Frankfurt.
- Q3: Release AcmeDeploy 2.0 with built-in secret management.
- Q4: Introduce usage-based pricing for AcmeHost.
