# Lending Policy (Synthetic)

## LEND-1 Debt-to-Income
LEND-1.1 Debt-to-income ratio (DTI) = (existing monthly debt + estimated new loan payment) / gross monthly income.
LEND-1.2 DTI above 43% must be declined.
LEND-1.3 DTI between 36% and 43% may be approved only with a credit score of 700 or higher; otherwise refer.

## LEND-2 Credit Score Bands
LEND-2.1 Score of 700 or higher: eligible for auto-approval if all other checks pass.
LEND-2.2 Score from 680 to 699: eligible for approval when DTI is 36% or less.
LEND-2.3 Score from 580 to 679: refer to manual underwriting.
LEND-2.4 Score below 580: decline.

## LEND-3 Credit History
LEND-3.1 Applicants with less than 2 years of credit history are thin-file and must be referred to manual underwriting regardless of score.
LEND-3.2 Thin-file applicants may be approved by an underwriter with a lower loan amount or a co-signer.

## LEND-4 Collateral
LEND-4.1 For secured loans, loan-to-value (LTV) above 90% must be referred.
LEND-4.2 LTV above 100% must be declined.
