# Enhanced Due Diligence, PEPs and Monitoring

## 1. Politically Exposed Persons
Enhanced due diligence (EDD) is required for politically exposed persons (PEPs), their family members and close associates. EDD includes senior management approval before onboarding, establishing the source of wealth and source of funds, and enhanced ongoing monitoring.

## 2. High-Risk Jurisdictions
EDD must also be applied to customers connected with countries identified by FATF as high-risk or subject to increased monitoring.

## 3. Ongoing Monitoring and Suspicious Activity
Transactions must be monitored against the customer risk profile. Unusual or suspicious activity must be escalated and a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filed with the Financial Intelligence Unit within the regulatory deadline. Customers must not be tipped off.
