# Customer Due Diligence and Onboarding Policy

## 1. Customer Identification
Banks must identify every customer before establishing a business relationship. Individuals must provide a government-issued photo ID and proof of address dated within the last three months. Identity must be verified using reliable, independent source documents or electronic verification.

## 2. Standard CDD Requirements
Customer Due Diligence (CDD) includes identifying the customer, verifying identity, understanding the purpose and intended nature of the account, and conducting ongoing monitoring. Records must be retained for at least five years after the relationship ends.

## 3. Simplified Due Diligence
Simplified due diligence may apply to low-risk customers such as listed public companies or government bodies, subject to a documented risk assessment.
