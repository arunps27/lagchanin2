# Beneficial Ownership Procedure

## 1. UBO Threshold
For legal entities, banks must identify each ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) who directly or indirectly owns 25% or more of the equity interests. Where no natural person meets the threshold, the bank must identify the senior managing official.

## 2. Verification of Corporate Customers
Obtain the certificate of incorporation, articles of association, register of directors and a shareholder register. Complex ownership structures involving trusts or shell companies require additional documentation and escalation to compliance.
