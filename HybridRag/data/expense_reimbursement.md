# Travel and Expense Reimbursement
Employees may claim reasonable business expenses within 30 days of the expense date through the Expense app. Receipts are required for any item above 25 USD. Domestic hotel stays are capped at 220 USD per night and international stays at 300 USD per night. Meals are reimbursed up to 70 USD per day while traveling. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with director approval. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Claims above 2,000 USD require additional approval from the department head, and reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle.
