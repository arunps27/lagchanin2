# Leave Policy
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave per month, for a total of 21 days per year. Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any balance above 10 days expires on March 31. Sick leave is separate and grants 12 paid days per year, with a medical certificate required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Parental leave is 16 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for emergencies, and approved by the line manager.
